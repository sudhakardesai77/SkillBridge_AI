# 🥇 SkillBridge AI

## 🚀 Career Intelligence Platform for Students

SkillBridge AI is an AI-powered career intelligence platform designed to help students prepare for placements and improve their chances of getting jobs.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Students often apply for jobs without knowing:

- Which companies they are eligible for
- Which skills they are missing
- Whether their resume is ATS-friendly
- How to prepare for interviews
- How to track their placement preparation

---

## 💡 Solution

SkillBridge AI acts as a personal placement assistant that helps students analyze their skills, check eligibility, improve their resumes, prepare for interviews, and discover job opportunities.
